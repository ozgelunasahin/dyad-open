The Writing Practice

Three months of stamina that builds the CHT-fellow profile, feeds the launch, and costs nothing extra: the daily rep, the weekly essay, the monthly flagship. Seth Godin has published every single day for over fifteen years — and most of his posts are under 200 words. The stamina is the noticing, not the word count.

started 15 July 2026 · target: mid-October · the streak is the deliverable

The three cadences

daily · 10–15 minutes · 100–250 words

The rep

One observation per day from what you are already living: a governance decision, a member's sentence (with consent), a line from a call, a build-log note. Publish it — Substack notes or the build log; the venue matters less than the streak.

The floor: three sentences still counts. A bad day that ships three sentences is a kept day.

weekly · one fixed 2–3 hour slot · 800–1,200 words

The essay

Twelve essays in twelve weeks, every one extracted from work already done. Writing is extraction, not invention. Titles below.

monthly · aimed outward

The flagship

Month 1: the positioning essay — "Human meeting infrastructure in the age of AI companions." This is the CHT thesis, written a year before the application.

Month 2: pitch it as an op-ed. First-choice venues: New_Public, then Netzpolitik or Tagesspiegel Background (German reach), Every (builder audience). One acceptance checks the "published work" box.

Month 3: the research-shaped piece — the ATProto experiment findings, or "90 days of consent-based governance: what actually happened," with real numbers from the decision log. This becomes the writing sample for the next cohort.

weekly · one outreach email · target 2–3 recorded by october

The podcast lane

Team Human (Douglas Rushkoff) is the thematic bullseye; then the ATProto/fediverse circuit, Berlin startup shows, New_Public's audio. The Greg and Rudy relationships are warm introductions into exactly these rooms.

The rules

1 · This is not extra work. The daily rep is the documentary layer of the social strategy; the essays are the zine and the launch content; the flagship is the fellowship application. One practice, four goals. If it ever feels like a fifth job, the practice is set up wrong — shrink it, don't skip it.
2 · Streak over quality, tracked in public. The commitment is to the calendar, not the muse. "Day 47 of writing daily" is itself content — and it is precisely the "active media presence" the fellowship screens for.
3 · Godin's ratios. Daily and small beats weekly and grand. Write like you talk. Ship before ready. The quality shows up on its own around week six; do not look for it before then.
