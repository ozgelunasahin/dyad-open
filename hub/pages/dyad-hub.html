Avant Now Accelerator — Cohort 11 (Berlin)

impact-first accelerator · up to 12 months, full-time in person in Berlin · connect by 31 July 2026 (via F6S)

Berlin's impact-first, tech-forward accelerator. Application: eligibility check, written questions (problem, differentiation, validation, business model, go-to-market, impact KPIs, team, setback story), pitch deck, and a one-minute unscripted founder video on YouTube (their video rules are pure YC: no production, no memorized script, audible audio).

Three gates to check before spending an evening: majority female-led founder team; NO revenues or funding yet (if the SAB angel round closes first, this may disqualify — timing matters); MVP-ready (dyad clears this easily). Read their extended 7-page eligibility document first, and ask them directly where an in-progress angel round stands.

Next: confirm the female-led and no-funding-yet gates against dyad's actual cap table and round timing; if clear, the HTW pitch materials and 1-minute video do double duty here — one recording, two applications.

IBB GründungsBONUS Plus (Berlin)

cost subsidy · 50% of eligible costs, up to €50,000 · disbursed over up to 2 years

Berlin state funding for innovative, sustainable startups with technological, digital, creative, or social business models. Covers operating costs (rent, marketing), personnel (up to €2,000/month per founder, max half the subsidy), IT development, and consulting. Founders must hold majority shares and manage the company. Only costs not yet incurred at application count.

The gate: the company must be no older than 18 months at application. Check dyad's incorporation date before anything else — this decides whether the door is open at all.

Deep research (15 Jul): currently open, applications „ab sofort“; scored 5/5, the cleanest non-dilutive Berlin match. Position dyad as a Berlin social/digital startup with strong sustainability and privacy characteristics, not as a thin-tech app. Next: verify entity age (≤18 months), then apply via ibb-bt.antragsverwaltung.de.

The Migrant Accelerator — Generator HdM (Stuttgart)

accelerator · 12 weeks, online + kick-off and demo day in person · €2,000 allowance · no equity, no fees · deadline 9 August 2026, cohort 2 Sep – 26 Nov

EXIST-supported program at Hochschule der Medien Stuttgart for first- and second-generation migrant founders based in Germany. Requires a team of at least two — Luna and Theodore qualify as a pair. Offers intercultural mentorship, German market and PR support, growth coaching, and community networks, run entirely in English.

Stage framing: they want prototype to early MVP. dyad is past MVP, so pitch the community-stack layer (Phase 2 of the deck) or the founding-member launch as the thing being accelerated, not the core app.

Next: apply before 9 August — free, no equity, and the German-market PR support lands exactly in the launch quarter. Weekly sessions are online; only kick-off and demo day need travel to Stuttgart.

EXIST Gründerstipendium (federal)

stipend · €2,500–3,000 per founder per month for 12 months + €30,000 materials + €5,000 coaching · via a university

The strongest founder-runway program in Germany, for knowledge-based ideas anchored through a university or research institution. Team of up to three; normally no more than one person with a degree older than five years.

The gate that decides everything: the company must not yet be founded at funding start. If dyad is already incorporated, this door is closed; if not, it is wide open with a Berlin university anchor.

Next: test eligibility with a Berlin startup centre or transfer office, using the open-source AT Protocol integration, privacy architecture, and governance stack as the knowledge-based novelty claim.

Center for Humane Technology — Emerging Voices Fellowship

fellowship · $30,000 stipend · 6 months, 15h/week, remote · inaugural cohort closed 12 July 2026

For mid-career voices on AI and society: researchers, journalists, civil society, tech insiders with published work and media presence. Deliverables: one research output, op-eds, a podcast episode. CHT's thesis is dyad's thesis: harms are predictable products of business models and design choices.

Two catches: this round's deadline has passed, and the frame is AI — dyad would enter as the counter-example (human meeting infrastructure in the AI-companion era), not as an AI project. Also 15h/week is half a founder's week during launch months.

Next: build the fellow profile through the launch itself (zine, build log, op-eds, podcast appearances are the exact requirements), then apply to the next cohort with the AI-era-loneliness angle.

model, not a fund

The CoTech model — earning, not raising

UK network of worker co-ops · funded by client work · network sustained in-kind by members

CoTech's tech co-operatives raise nothing: they sell services, keep the surplus (no shareholders), and maintain their shared network through in-kind contributions (one co-op builds the site, another hosts, another does design). Since 2024 they are the tech arm of the workers.coop federation.

What dyad can take: a small services leg selling what already exists — the ATProto sign-in bridge, the governance documents suite (CC BY-SA invites exactly this), the membership system, the trust & safety framework — to other communities and co-ops. A day or two a week of this funds the founder faster than most grants on this page, without touching the product's independence. And in-kind federation: joining commons networks (Democratic Tech Fund's federation, CoTech-style structures in DE) can cover hosting, design, and referrals without money changing hands.

Next: list the three sellable assets with a day rate; mention the services leg in the Democratic Tech Fund conversation, since their federation is where the first clients are.

  1. Grants fund the work; membership funds the organization. Never let a grant redefine the product.
  2. Prefer funders whose thesis matches the architecture — those applications write themselves from existing documents.
  3. One application in flight at a time; each reuses the same narrative: offline-first, data-minimal, member-governed, open source.
  4. Three legs, in order of dignity: earning (services, memberships), then relationships (federations, in-kind), then raising (grants, angels). The CoTech co-ops live on leg one alone.
