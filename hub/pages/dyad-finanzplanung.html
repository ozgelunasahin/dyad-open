9 · Liquiditätsplan, structure for the Excel

„Im Liquiditätsplan ist der Abruf der Zuwendung … anteilig in maximal 4 Tranchen zu berücksichtigen."

  • Cadence. Monthly for the first business year, quarterly after. The opening balance is the Stammkapital of 3.000 plus Eigenmittel of 20.000.
  • Inflows. Membership revenue with a monthly ramp from September to December 2026 of roughly 500, 1.500, 3.000, and 4.200 EUR with founding one-offs on top. Grant tranches of 10.000 in December 2026, 25.000 split across 2027, and 15.000 in 2028. Pre-seed capital at closing, which the go to market plan schedules between October 2026 and February 2027 ⟨enter the closing date once known; count only what is signed⟩.
  • Outflows. The cost table from section 1, spread monthly, with salaries starting in the month hiring starts and eligible spending paced behind the cash actually in the account.
  • The line IBB reads first. The balance must never go negative, and the months before each grant tranche are the stress points because of the Erstattungsprinzip. Without any pre-seed money the draft still closes, because spending follows revenue; with the round closed, the buffer widens and hiring can move earlier.
